## 3. `hotel_booking_demand_pii/original/hotel_booking.csv`

- **Rows:** 119,390 &nbsp; **Columns:** 36 &nbsp; **Size:** 23.95 MB
- **Duplicate rows:** 0
- All 32 business columns are byte-identical in distribution to dataset 1
  (same means, same category breakdowns), **plus 4 synthetic PII columns**:

| Column | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| name | text (5,000 distinct, cycled over 119,390 rows) | fabricated full name |
| email | text (5,000 distinct) | fabricated, pattern `First_Last@domain` |
| phone-number | text (5,000 distinct) | fabricated `###-###-####` |
| credit_card | text (5,000 distinct) | **masked** `************####` (last 4 digits only) |

The 5,000-distinct-value ceiling on `name`/`email`/`phone-number`/`credit_card`
(vs. 119,390 rows) shows these were **generated once and cycled/repeated**,
not real per-booking guest records — synthetic augmentation added on top of
the same base dataset as #1/#2. `agent`/`company`/`adr` also cap at 5,000
distinct in every variant, confirming a shared profiler artifact (distinct-value
sampling was capped at 5,000 in this discovery run) — but for `name`/`email`
specifically the *underlying data itself* also repeats within that cap
(e.g. "Jamie Smith" appears 4 times, `MRogers@mail.com` appears 2 times),
which is the real signal of synthetic reuse, not a profiler limit.

## 6. `hotel_bookings_variants/original/hotel_bookings_updated_2024.csv`

- **Rows:** 119,390 &nbsp; **Columns:** 33 &nbsp; **Size:** 20.29 MB
- **Duplicate rows:** 0
- Same 119,390 rows and identical per-column statistics to dataset 1
  (`adr` mean 101.83, `is_canceled` mean 0.370, etc.) but with two columns
  **rewritten**:
  - `hotel` → replaced with a fabricated `"<City Hotel|Resort Hotel> - <Indian city>"`
    string across 30 values (15 Indian cities × 2 hotel types)
  - `city` → new column, the 15 Indian city names alone
  - `arrival_date_year` forced to a constant `2024`
  - `reservation_status_date` → rewritten as full 2024 timestamps with
    microsecond precision (`2024-10-06 09:58:46.606303764`)

  This is a **synthetic re-labeling** of dataset 1 (same underlying rows,
  same relationships), not real 2024 bookings or real city-level data.
